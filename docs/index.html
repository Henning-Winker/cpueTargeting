cpueTargeting

Simulators for CPUE targeting and standardization

Description of the simulation models

The spatial multispecies simulator (simulate_bathymetric(), after Thorson et al. 2017 and Ono et al. 2015), its extension to a longline tuna fishery with vertical targeting (simulate_tuna()), and the standardisation models (Cluster, DPC, HBF).

Targeting along a continuous gradient: the bathymetric example

The guideline (Run 22) simulator: Base, spatial, Cluster and DPC models, what the principal components and clusters capture, and replicates.

Tuna longline targeting: flat versus contrasting true trends

Spatial and vertical (HBF) targeting with identical targeting but flat or diverging true trends: when the catch composition captures targeting, and when it also absorbs abundance.

Analysing real catch and effort data: a step-by-step guide

The toolbox from the raw logbook to the standardised index, with guidelines for each decision: fleet and species selection, targeting covariates, models, diagnostics.

Setting up the logbook simulator: a user guide

Habitats, tactics, specialist and mixed vessels, mixed trips and reporting in simulate_logbook(), and what the PCs and clusters capture against the truth.

Standardising a realistic logbook

Blackspot seabream, conger and dolphinfish: selecting tactics and indicator vessels, choosing the error distribution, and predictive checks of zeros and tails by year.
